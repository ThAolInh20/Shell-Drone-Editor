# Danh sach Video Preview Can Quay Cho He Thong Hieu Ung Phao Hoa

Tai lieu nay liet ke toan bo danh sach cac video clip can quay de su dung cho he thong **Hover Video Preview Tooltip** va **Firework Designer Studio**.

---

## 1. Thong So Ky Thuat Video Chuan

- **Dinh dang**: `.mp4` (H.264 / AVC) hoac `.webm`
- **Ti le & Kich thuoc**: 1:1 Vuong, **480 x 480 px** (hoac toi thieu 320 x 320 px)
- **Thoi luong**: **2.0s den 3.5s** (tu luc bung no den khi hat tan het)
- **Toc do khung hinh**: **30 fps** (hoac 60 fps)
- **Am thanh**: Khong co audio (Muted / No audio track)
- **Nen**: Nen den `#000000` hoac `#0a0c14`
- **Dung luong muc tieu**: 150 KB - 400 KB moi video

## 6. Huong Dan Quy Trinh Quay va Xuat File

1. **Ghi hinh**: Su dung cong cu quay man hinh (OBS, Windows Game Bar `Win + G`, hoac chuc nang Export clip) voi khung vuong 480x480.
2. **Dat ten file**: Copy chinh xac ten file tu cot **Ten File Video** o cac bang tren (chu thuong, khong dau, noi bang dau `-` hoac `_`).
3. **Luu file**: Copy cac file `.mp4` da quay vao dung thu muc con ben trong `./public/previews/`.
4. **Kiem tra**: Mo phan mem, di chuot (hover) vao tung tuy chon trong Property Inspector hoac Studio, video se tu dong phat mui preview.
